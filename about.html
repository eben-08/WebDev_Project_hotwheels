ABOUT US

A Passion for
Exceptional Automobiles

Founded in Dublin, Ireland, Wheels on Fire is an independent automotive collection dedicated to celebrating exceptional vehicles, outstanding craftsmanship and the passion behind the world's most remarkable cars.

OUR STORY

Built on Passion,
Driven by Excellence

Wheels on Fire was founded in Dublin in 2015 by John O'Reilly, whose lifelong passion for automobiles inspired him to establish a company dedicated to exceptional vehicles.

What began as a small private collection gradually developed into an automotive business focused on bringing together some of the most distinctive cars from around the world.

During its early years, Wheels on Fire concentrated on building its collection and developing relationships with fellow car enthusiasts.

As interest in the collection grew, the company expanded its focus to helping customers discover vehicles that matched their individual preferences and lifestyles.

OUR JOURNEY

From a Small Collection
to a Growing Vision

Since its establishment, Wheels on Fire has continued to develop and adapt to the changing automotive industry.

Originally focused on a limited number of privately collected vehicles, the company gradually expanded its collection to include a wider selection of exceptional automobiles.

With the growing importance of digital technology, Wheels on Fire introduced its online platform, allowing visitors to explore its collection and discover information about different vehicles.

Today, Wheels on Fire continues to combine its passion for cars with a modern approach to automotive discovery.

OUR FOUNDER

John O'Reilly

John O'Reilly founded Wheels on Fire with a vision of creating a place where automotive passion and exceptional design come together.

His appreciation for engineering, craftsmanship and the history of automobiles has played an important role in shaping the company's identity.

Under his direction, Wheels on Fire continues to develop its collection and share its enthusiasm for remarkable vehicles.

LOOKING TO THE FUTURE

Our Vision

Our vision is to establish Wheels on Fire as a recognised destination for automotive enthusiasts in Ireland and beyond.

We aim to continue expanding our collection, improving our online experience and helping visitors discover vehicles that reflect their individual interests.
